Vampire Blocks

Click to play. You are a vampire.
W A S D move · Space jump / swim up · Mouse look
Short click or right click: place a block, use an item, or open a table · Hold left click: break a block or bite a mob · 1-9 or wheel: pick slot · E: open all blocks · M: spawn a random mob
K: save your project (email, browser, or copy code).
Water, lava, and milk buckets spray liquid anywhere you aim (short click, never runs out). Empty bucket scoops water or lava.
Ladder: stand in it and hold Space or W to climb, Shift or S to go down.
Ice portal: build a frame of Ice blocks (3x4 or 4x5) and light it with Flint and Steel to reach the Frigid Zone.
End portal: a 5x5 ring of 12 End Portal Frames (no corners), put an Eye of Ender in each.
Creative mode: no health, no hunger, no damage. Sand and gravel fall, ores hide underground.
Build a 3×4 or 4×5 obsidian rectangle outline (from the inventory) then light it with Flint and Steel (craft one at a crafting table) and step in.
Perks: fast, high jumps, night vision. No sunlight damage. Go anywhere, any time, and bite mobs to heal.
